# MarketPulse

## Vision

MarketPulse is a platform that collects, standardizes and analyzes fresh produce market prices from South African fresh produce markets.

---

## Phase 1 Goals

- Connect to Tshwane Fresh Produce Market
- Connect to Cape Town Fresh Produce Market
- Store daily prices
- Build a historical database
- Build a dashboard for buyers and vendors
