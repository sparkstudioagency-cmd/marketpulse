# Business Glossary

## Product

The produce itself.

Examples:
- Tomato
- Potato
- Lemon

A product does not include packaging, grade or weight.

---

## Container

The packaging or presentation used by the market.

Examples:
- Carton
- BI
- NN
- Punnet

---

## Grade

The quality classification assigned by the market.

Examples:
- Class 1
- AA
- Export

---

## Market

An official South African Fresh Produce Market.

Examples:
- Tshwane
- Cape Town

---

## Daily Price

A published market record representing one product configuration on one day.
